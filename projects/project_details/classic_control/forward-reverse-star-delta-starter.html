Forward/Reverse Star-Delta Starter

CADe SIMUElectrical SchematicThree-Phase Motor

Project Overview

This project presents the power and control schematic for a forward/reverse star-delta starter for a three-phase induction motor, designed in CADe SIMU. The circuit uses forward/reverse contactors, a star-delta timer, push buttons, overload protection, and contactor interlocking.

Operating Principle

  1. Press the Forward or Reverse push button to energize the selected direction circuit, provided the stop and safety contacts are closed.
  2. The star contactor connects the motor windings in star during the initial starting stage.
  3. After the timer delay, the star contactor releases and the circuit changes over to delta operation.
  4. The Stop button, Emergency Stop, or overload protection interrupts the control circuit and releases the contactors.

Control Circuit

Main Components

  • MCCB / MCB
  • KM1 — Forward contactor
  • KM2 — Reverse contactor
  • KM3 / KM4 — Star-delta contactors

Technical Notes

Reversing Safety Note

Stop the motor and allow it to come to a complete stop before reversing direction. Reversing while it is still spinning can create high electrical and mechanical stress, potentially damaging the motor and contactors.

Recommended improvement: Add a suitable time-delay relay or dedicated reversing control system to prevent reverse operation until sufficient stopping time has elapsed. Select the delay for the motor and application. A timer alone does not confirm that the motor has actually stopped; the stopping time must be properly determined and verified.

Engineering Considerations

This is an educational schematic, not a validated design for a real installation. All equipment ratings, protection, interlocking, and timing must be checked for the actual motor and application.

  • Choose protection and contactors based on the motor nameplate, circuit arrangement, prospective fault current, manufacturer guidance, and applicable standards.
  • Confirm the motor is suitable for star-delta starting at the available supply voltage.
  • Verify the electrical and mechanical interlocks, overload protection, earthing, and isolation before any real-world implementation.
